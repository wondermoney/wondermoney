ETFs allow you to buy a diversified basket of assets in a single trade. For most beginners, broad-market ETFs are a simple way to participate in long-term market growth without picking individual stocks.

Focus on three fundamentals: diversification, cost, and consistency. A low expense ratio and a regular contribution schedule usually matter more than trying to time the market.

Pick an allocation you can hold through market drops, then rebalance periodically. The best portfolio is the one you can maintain for years.
