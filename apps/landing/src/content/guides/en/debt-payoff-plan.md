Start by listing every debt: balance, interest rate, minimum payment, and due date. Continue paying all minimums, then direct every extra euro or dollar toward one target debt.

Choose a method that fits your psychology. Avalanche saves more interest by prioritizing highest rates, while snowball builds momentum by clearing smaller balances first. Both work when followed consistently.

Review your progress monthly and celebrate milestones. Visible progress keeps motivation high and helps transform debt repayment from a burden into a plan.
