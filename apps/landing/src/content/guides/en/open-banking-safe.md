Open Banking lets you securely share account data with licensed apps through bank APIs. You grant explicit consent and can usually revoke access from your banking app or provider dashboard.

Good security habits still matter: use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and review connected apps regularly. Only connect trusted providers with clear privacy policies.

When used correctly, Open Banking reduces manual work and improves visibility without sharing your bank credentials directly with third-party tools.
