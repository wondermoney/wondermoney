Nominal returns can look strong while purchasing power still falls. Real return adjusts performance for inflation, giving a more honest view of financial progress.

For long-term planning, always compare expected portfolio growth against inflation assumptions. This helps you set realistic goals for retirement, education, and major expenses.

Taxes and fees can further reduce effective return. Tracking net, inflation-adjusted results is one of the best ways to improve decision quality.
