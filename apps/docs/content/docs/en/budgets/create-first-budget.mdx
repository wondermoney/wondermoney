title: Create your first budget

The budget wizard helps you choose a household setup, pick the categories that matter, and assign monthly targets. Start with a simple budget that matches the way you actually spend.

## Start with envelopes, not perfection

Think of each category as an envelope with a monthly amount inside it. The first version only needs to be realistic enough to guide decisions, not perfect on day one.

## Before you start

Have your main household expenses and a recent month of spending in mind. You can refine the budget later, so the first version only needs the categories you will use now.

## Steps

1. Open **Budgets** and choose **Create budget** to start the budget wizard.
2. Choose who the budget is for: **Household** or **Personal**.
3. Confirm the household size and dependent details.
4. Add your income. The budget is created when you continue from this step.
5. Choose the categories you want to track.
6. Set a monthly amount for each category, then choose **Finish**.

## Household or personal budget

A **household** budget is shared: everyone in the household sees it, and members and admins can change it. A **personal** budget is yours alone. Nobody else in the household sees it, including the household's owner and admins: it is not in their budget lists, reports, alerts, emails, insights or assistant answers, and it is not in their data exports.

- A personal budget counts the transactions you can see: the household's shared accounts and your own private accounts. You can link your private accounts to it; a household budget only links shared accounts.
- You choose when you create the budget, and it can't be switched afterwards. Personal budgets carry a **Personal** label, and the budget list shows them apart from the household's budgets.
- Viewers can't create budgets of either kind.

## Suggest from your spending

If you already have transactions, choose **Suggest from my spending** on the categories step. For each category it shows the middle month of your last three complete months of spending, rounded up to the nearest 5. Categories with less than two months of history are marked instead of suggested.

Use **Apply** to take one suggestion or **Apply all** to take every suggestion. You can still edit any amount afterwards.

## Review

After saving the budget, compare the monthly totals with your real spending. If a category feels too broad or too narrow, adjust it before you rely on the numbers.

## Common issues

- Too many categories make the budget hard to maintain.
- Household details do not match the people who share expenses.
- Monthly amounts were entered as yearly totals by mistake.
- A category was added even though it rarely appears in your spending.
