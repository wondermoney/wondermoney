---
title: Adjust or Delete a Budget
description: Change a budget after you create it, or remove it when it is no longer needed.
---

## When to adjust a budget

Adjust a budget when your spending pattern changes.

Common reasons include:

- A category needs a higher limit
- Income changed and you want to rebalance
- You split one budget into several goals
- You added new recurring spending

## When to delete a budget

Delete a budget only when you no longer need it.

Use delete if:

- The budget was created by mistake
- You moved to a new budgeting method
- The budget duplicates another one

## Before you change anything

Check the current month first.

Review:

- Category limits
- Assigned amounts
- Spending already tracked
- Any linked goals or planning notes

If the budget is already in use, adjusting it is usually safer than deleting it.

## How to adjust a budget

1. Open Budgets.
2. Select the budget you want to change.
3. Update the category limits or totals.
4. Save your changes.
5. Review the updated summary.

After saving, confirm that the new limits match your intent.

To rename a budget or change its settings (the day its months start on, and whether overspending carries into the next month), on the web open the budget's menu in the budgets list and choose **Edit**. In the mobile app, the same settings are on the **Edit budget** screen.

## Suggest a new limit from your spending

When you edit a category, the editor shows a suggestion from your own spending beside the current amount. It uses the middle of your last three complete months, rounded up to the nearest 5, and leaves out transfers, loan principal and goal funding. Choose **Apply** to fill the amount, then save. If the category has less than two months of history, the editor says so instead of suggesting a number.

The suggestion only fills the amount. **Apply change to following months** still decides whether the saved amount reaches later months.

## How to delete a budget

1. On the web, open **Budgets**, open the budget's menu in the list and choose **Delete**. In the mobile app, open **Edit budget** and choose **Delete budget**.
2. Review the confirmation message.
3. Confirm deletion.

Deleting removes the budget's categories and planned amounts. Your transactions are not affected.

## Next year's budget

A budget carries on into the next year. From 1 December, next year's months are ready: each one starts with the categories, amounts, recurrence and linked accounts of this December. From December, the next-month arrow opens January.

When you change a category with **Apply change to following months**, the change also reaches the months of next year that already exist.

## Common mistakes

- Changing the wrong month
- Deleting a budget instead of editing it
- Leaving a category without a limit by mistake
- Forgetting to review linked spending plans

## What to do after the change

Check the budget overview again.

Confirm that:

- Category totals still add up
- Monthly progress looks correct
- Any alerts or warnings make sense
- The budget reflects your current plan
