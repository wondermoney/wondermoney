---
title: Compare two months
description: See what changed between two months, category by category.
---

The month comparison puts two months side by side: what came in, what went out, and how each spending category moved between them. Use it to answer "why was last month different?" without re-reading every transaction.

## Pick the two months

The report has two pickers. **Month** is the month you are examining; **Compared with** is the month it is measured against. They start on this month and last month, and you can choose any two months independently.

## Read the totals

The totals table shows income, expenses, net and savings rate for both months, plus the change between them.

- **Change** is the first month minus the second. A positive change in expenses means you spent more in the month you are examining.
- **Savings rate** is the share of income you kept. A dash means the month had no income, so the rate cannot be stated.

## Read the categories

Each category has a bar showing its change and a row with its spending in both months. Bars point right when a category grew and left when it fell; the table is ordered by the size of the change, so the biggest movements come first.

- A category that appears in only one month is still compared, against zero: a new category is a rise and a vanished one a fall.
- A change with no percentage means the category had no spending in the comparison month, so there is no baseline to measure against.
- Select an amount to open that month's transactions for the category.

## What counts

The comparison uses the same rules as the cash flow and spending reports, so its totals match theirs for the same filters.

- A refund is money in, not negative spending: it lowers the net but not the category total.
- A transfer between your own accounts is neither income nor spending, unless you turn on **Include transfers**.
- Pending transactions are left out unless you turn on **Include pending**.
- Money in a currency with no exchange rate yet is left out of the totals. A note under the report says how many transactions were left out and in which currencies.

## Export

Select the export icon to download the comparison as a CSV file. It carries the month totals and one row per category, with the same figures the report shows.
