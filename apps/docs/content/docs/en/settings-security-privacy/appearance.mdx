## What you can change

- **Theme**: **Light** or **Dark**. The mobile app also has **System**, which follows your device settings.
- **Language**: English, Portuguese (Portugal) or Spanish. The mobile app also has **Device language**.

On the web, open **Settings** and choose **Appearance**. In the mobile app, open **More > Settings** and choose **Appearance**.

## How the setting works

Appearance changes apply immediately across the app. With **System** in the mobile app, WonderMoney switches automatically when your device theme changes. The theme is remembered on each device and browser separately.

## Language and your emails

The language you pick in Appearance, on the web or in the mobile app, is also your account language: your emails go out in it, and an email that is not translated yet arrives in English. When you sign up, your account takes the language of the sign-up page, or the language the mobile app is set to. The email that confirms your address at sign-up goes out in that language too.

## When to use it

Choose a different appearance if text is hard to read, screens feel too bright, or you want the app to match your device.

## What to check

- Whether the change applies to the current device only.
- Whether your browser or device is overriding the chosen theme.
- Whether a refresh is needed after saving the preference.

## What to try if the change is not visible

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check the browser zoom and system display settings.
3. Sign out and back in if the preference still does not apply.
