## What this section covers

- Profile preferences and appearance
- Email preferences
- Password changes
- Two-factor authentication
- Assistant consent and settings
- Data privacy and account deletion
- Bank access and connected services

## Where to go

- Use **Security** for password, 2FA and passkey changes.
- Use **Notifications** to control which email categories you receive and to turn them all off at once.
- Use **Assistant** for assistant consent, launcher visibility, pending changes and the audit log.
- Use **Privacy** to hide AI features, export or restore your data, and delete your account.
- Use **Connections** to review the read-only bank access tied to your account.
