WonderMoney asks for your consent before it turns on the assistant, and you can change that at any time from settings. Browser analytics has a separate opt-in choice, saved in this browser and changeable in **Settings → Privacy**.

## How consent is collected

Creating an account means accepting the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy: the web sign-up page says so under the form, and the mobile app has a box to tick. Analytics consent is separate from creating an account.

The AI assistant has its own consent: it stays off until you select **Enable assistant** on its consent screen, and that choice is stored with your account, so it applies wherever you sign in.

The wonder.money website separately asks about analytics and advertising measurement. On your first visit, the **Privacy choices** banner offers **Use necessary only**, **Customize** and **Allow all**; **Analytics** and **Advertising** stay off unless you allow them. The choice is saved in that browser, not in your account, so each browser asks once. To change it, select the **Privacy choices** button in the corner of any page of the website.

The web app loads browser analytics only when the operator configures it and you opt in. You can withdraw that choice in **Settings → Privacy**. A self-hosted server has no browser analytics by default (see [Analytics](/en/docs/self-hosting/configure#analytics)). This choice does not change the separately configured API/worker analytics outbox or mobile telemetry; the mobile app sends usage events only while connected to the server it was built for.

## What you can control

- Whether assistant features are enabled for your account.
- Whether the assistant launcher is visible.
- Whether the web app may use analytics in this browser.
- Whether the wonder.money website may use analytics and advertising measurement in your browser.

## How to change your decision

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Assistant**.
2. Turn **Assistant enabled** on or off. Turn on **Hide launcher** to keep the assistant available without its launcher.

A change is saved as soon as you make it. To turn off every AI feature at once, use **Settings > Privacy > Hide AI features**.

If a change does not appear to apply, sign out and sign back in, then check the settings again.

## What consent does not allow

Consent for assistant features does not permit WonderMoney to move money, initiate transactions, or change bank records. The assistant can only read the data exposed to assistant features.
