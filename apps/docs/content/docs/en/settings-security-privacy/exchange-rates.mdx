---
title: Automatic exchange rates
description: How WonderMoney keeps currency conversions up to date without manual setup.
---

WonderMoney fetches European Central Bank reference rates automatically on working days. You do not need to enter exchange rates in the web app.

## What updates automatically

After a successful rate refresh, the worker recalculates estimated or unconverted transaction amounts and foreign-currency account balances in the background. Reports and net worth use the available rates when they are requested.

## When a rate is missing

The feed covers a limited set of currencies. A missing currency or historical date can leave an amount unconverted; the original amount remains visible and the total explains what was left out. An older available rate may be marked as estimated.
