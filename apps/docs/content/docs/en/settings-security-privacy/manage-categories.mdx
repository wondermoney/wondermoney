## What you can manage

- Create a category of your own with **Add Category**: a name, a color, an icon and, if you want, a parent category.
- Edit a category of your own: its name, color, icon and parent category.
- Delete a category of your own.
- Choose whether a category **Counts as spending**. When this is off, rows in the category are left out of spending in reports, budgets and the home cash flow.

System categories cannot be edited or deleted. You can still choose whether one counts as spending.

## How category changes work

An edit applies everywhere at once: if you rename a category or change its color or icon, the change appears wherever that category is shown, on past transactions too. The form warns you when a name or a color is already in use.

Deleting a category removes it from the category list, so it can no longer be chosen for a transaction, a budget or a rule. It changes nothing else:

- Transactions that had the category are not moved to another one. Recategorize them yourself, before or after.
- Budgets that plan an amount for the category are not edited.
- Rules that set the category are kept, but they stop setting it.

On the web, the delete action takes effect as soon as you select it, without a confirmation step.

## Before you edit categories

- Confirm you are in the right account.
- Check whether the category is already attached to budgets or imported transactions.
- Decide whether you want to rename or delete the category.

## What to do

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Categories**. In the mobile app, open **More > Settings** and choose **Categories**.
2. Select **Add Category**, or the edit action next to the category you want to change.
3. Apply the edit and save it.
4. Verify the category in a transaction or budget screen.

## Common mistakes

- Creating duplicate categories with slightly different names.
- Deleting a category that is still used in budgets or summaries.
- Renaming a category without checking imported data.
