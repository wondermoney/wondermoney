---
title: Privacy mode
description: Hide balances and amounts on screen when other people can see it.
---

Privacy mode hides every amount in WonderMoney behind a fixed mask (`€•••••`), so you can open the app on a train, at work or while sharing your screen without showing what you have.

## Turn it on or off

- **Web, desktop:** select **Hide amounts** in the sidebar, under Help.
- **Web, phone or tablet:** select the eye button in the top bar, beside your profile.
- **Web, keyboard:** press **Shift+P** anywhere outside a text field.
- **Mobile app:** tap the eye button in the home header, or use the **Hide amounts** switch in **More → Settings → Appearance**.

Select the same control again (it now reads **Show amounts**) to bring the amounts back.

## What it hides

- Balances, totals and transaction amounts on every screen: home, accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, loans, portfolio, reports and bills & subscriptions.
- How many shares or units you hold or traded, in holdings, holding details and portfolio transactions. A quantity times the asset's public price is the value of the position.
- Chart axis values and the amounts in chart tooltips. Chart shapes stay, so you can still see trends.
- The gain or loss colour of a change, so the mask does not give away its direction.
- Screen readers hear "Hidden amount" instead of the value, and a hidden amount cannot be copied.

## What stays visible

- Percentages, counts and dates.
- Amount and quantity fields while you are editing them. A budget amount you can edit in place is masked until you select it.

## How the setting is remembered

Privacy mode is remembered on each device and browser separately. Turning it on in the mobile app does not turn it on in your browser, and the setting is not stored in your account.

Privacy mode only changes what is shown on screen. It does not change emails, exports or what the assistant can read.
