---
title: Read-only Bank Access
description: Understand what bank data WonderMoney can read and what it cannot change.
---

WonderMoney can read linked banking data to show balances, transactions, and account activity.

Use this access when you want account data in WonderMoney without giving the app permission to move money or edit bank records.

## What read-only means

Read-only access lets the app fetch data from your bank connection. It does not let WonderMoney move money or change records at your bank.

## Why this matters

- It supports transaction import.
- It supports account and cashflow views.
- It helps keep your financial data up to date.

If a bank connection stops updating, check the bank connection status first and then review the troubleshooting steps for that provider.
