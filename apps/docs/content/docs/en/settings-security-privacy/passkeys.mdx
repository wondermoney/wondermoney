---
title: Passkeys
description: Sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of typing your email and password.
---

A passkey lets you sign in without typing your email or password. Your device keeps the passkey, and you unlock it with your fingerprint, face or device PIN, a phone, or a security key.

Passkeys are an extra way to sign in. Your password, Google sign-in and Apple sign-in keep working, and you keep your password. You can't create an account with only a passkey.

Passkeys work on the web app. The mobile app doesn't support them yet.

## Add a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Security** and find the **Passkeys** section.
2. Select **Add a passkey**.
3. Follow your browser's prompt. It may ask for your fingerprint, face, device PIN, a phone or a security key.

Adding a passkey needs a recent sign-in. If you signed in more than a day ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

After you sign in with a password, the app offers once to add a passkey, if your browser supports passkeys and you have none yet. Select **Not now** to dismiss it; that browser won't ask again.

## Sign in with a passkey

On the login page, select **Sign in with a passkey**. In browsers that support passkey autofill, you can also choose a saved passkey from the suggestions on the email field.

There is no email or password to type.

## Passkeys and two-factor authentication

A passkey sign-in doesn't ask for your authenticator code. A passkey already proves two things: you have the device, and you unlocked it with your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Signing in with your password still asks for the code when two-factor authentication is on.

## Manage your passkeys

Each passkey in **Settings → Security → Passkeys** shows its name, when it was added (**Added** and a date) and when it was last used (**Last used** and a date, or **Never used**).

A new passkey takes the name of the password manager that stores it when the browser reports one, such as "Google Password Manager", "Windows Hello" or "1Password". Otherwise it is called "Passkey". Select **Rename** to give each passkey a name you can tell apart.

Select **Delete** to remove a passkey. It stops working in WonderMoney straight away, but it stays in your password manager or on your device until you remove it there too.

Personal API tokens can't list, add or remove passkeys.
