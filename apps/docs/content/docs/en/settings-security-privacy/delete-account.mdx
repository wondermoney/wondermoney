## Before you delete

- Download any records you want to keep.
- Confirm that you are signed in to the correct account.

## What happens when the account is deleted

Deleting the account removes your access to WonderMoney, revokes linked bank connections, stops WonderMoney emails and anonymizes your profile data. Some records may be retained where law or fraud prevention requires it. The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## How the flow works

On the web:

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Privacy**.
2. Select **Delete WonderMoney account** at the bottom of the tab.
3. Review the warning message.
4. Confirm the deletion only after you have checked the account details.

In the mobile app, open **More > Settings > Security** and go to **Delete account** at the bottom. Tick the acknowledgement, then select **Delete account permanently**.

## What to check if you do not see the option

- Whether account deletion is available for your current role.
- Whether you are in the correct signed-in session.
- Whether the page needs a refresh after changing settings.
