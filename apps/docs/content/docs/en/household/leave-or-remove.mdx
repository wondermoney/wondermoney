---
title: Leave or remove a member
description: Leave a household, remove someone from it, and understand what happens to the data.
---

Anyone except the owner can leave a household at any time. The owner can remove anyone else, and an admin can remove members and viewers. Either way, the household keeps its data.

## Leaving a household

As an admin, member or viewer, you can leave the household from its settings. After leaving, you no longer have access to the household's shared data, and your bank connections in it are disconnected. Your past accounts and transactions stay with the household.

Leaving does not delete your user account. What you lose is access to the household and its data. The next time you sign in, you are in a household of your own: the one you had before you joined, or a new, empty one.

## Removing someone

The owner can remove anyone else in the household. An admin can remove members and viewers, but not another admin or the owner.

<DocsImage
  src="/images/docs/household/leave-or-remove-remove-dialog.png"
  alt="The remove-member confirmation dialog, explaining that the person loses access and their bank connections are disconnected."
  caption="Removing someone asks you to confirm first."
  width={768}
  height={178}
/>

When you remove someone:

- They are signed out everywhere and lose access to the household right away.
- Their bank connections are disconnected, so the household stops syncing their banks.
- Their past accounts and transactions stay with the household.

Removing someone does not delete their user account. They keep their sign-in, and the next time they sign in they are in a household of their own.

## Private accounts of someone who leaves

When someone leaves or is removed, their private accounts stay in the household, shared as they were. The members an account was shared with keep their access, to view it or to edit it; nobody else gains access, not even the household owner. If the person joins the household again, they see their accounts again. See [When someone leaves or is removed](/en/docs/household/account-privacy#when-someone-leaves-or-is-removed).

## When the owner deletes their account

The owner can't leave the household; to hand it over, transfer ownership first (see [Transferring ownership](/en/docs/household/what-is-a-household#transferring-ownership)). If the owner deletes their account, the household is dissolved: everyone else loses access to the shared data, and their bank connections in it are disconnected.
