---
title: Ownership shares
description: Split an account between household members by percentage, so each member's net worth counts their part of it.
---

An account belongs to one owner, or to the whole household when it is joint (see [Filter by member](/en/docs/household/filter-by-member)). When two or more people really own it together, say a house fund held 60/40 or a mortgage split 50/50, you can give each member a percentage. Each member's net worth then counts their part of the account.

Ownership shares change **net worth only**. Spending reports, budgets and the transaction list keep following the account's owner, the joint switch and **Spent by**. They never change who can see the account: that is [account privacy](/en/docs/household/account-privacy).

In a household with only you, none of this appears.

## Split an account

1. Open the account's settings.
2. Under **Ownership**, turn on **Split between members**.
3. Enter a percentage for each member, with up to two decimals. The shares must add up to 100%.
4. Save.

To stop splitting, turn **Split between members** off and save. The account goes back to its owner, or to Shared when it is joint.

Only the account's owner or the household owner can change the shares. Everyone else who can see the account sees them read-only. You can give a share only to members who can see the account.

Shares are set on the web. The mobile app counts them in each member's net worth and shows, next to a split account, the percentage that is counted.

## How net worth counts it

- **No shares**: the account counts in full for its owner, or under Shared when it is joint.
- **With shares**: each member's net worth counts their percentage of the account's balance. A joint account with shares is split the same way, and counts nothing under Shared.
- Debts split the same way. A credit card or a mortgage kept as an account with a negative balance lowers each member's net worth by their share. A loan on the Loans page is not an account: it stays its owner's.
- The household's net worth doesn't change. Each member's figure plus Shared adds up to it exactly, across currencies: every account is converted into your currency first, then split.
- Net worth history uses today's shares for every date.
- A member's own figure only counts accounts they can see. An account hidden from them adds nothing to their figure, whatever share it gives them.

## When someone leaves

A share held by someone who has left the household stays on the account and counts under Shared, like the rest of their money. If they rejoin, it is theirs again. To give it to the members still in the household, enter new shares and save.

## Export and restore

A data export lists each account's shares. A share held by someone who has left appears without a member. A restore merges everyone into the person restoring, so a split account comes back wholly theirs.
