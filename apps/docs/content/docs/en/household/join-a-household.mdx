You join a household from the link in the invite email. If you are new to WonderMoney, you sign up from the link. If you already have an account, you accept the invite with it. Either way, you join with the role the person who invited you picked, and you use the email address the invite was sent to.

## Opening the invite

Open the link in the invite email. The page shows who invited you and which household you are joining. If the link has expired, been revoked, been replaced by a newer email or already been used, the page says so: ask the person who invited you to send it again.

## Joining as a new person

1. Open the invite link and choose to create your account.
2. Finish the signup. The email address is the one the invite was sent to.

Your new account joins the household straight away, with the role from the invite.

## Joining with an account you already have

1. Open the invite link while you are signed in, or sign in from it, with the account the invite was sent to.
2. Check the confirmation screen, which names the household you are joining, and confirm.

You can join only while your current household has no accounts or transactions. If it has any, you see a message saying so and nothing happens: nothing is merged or moved. To join, delete those accounts and transactions first, or accept the invite with another account.

When you join:

- Budgets, categories, rules, goals and anything else you set up in your old household stay there. They are not moved to the new household.
- Bank connections in your old household are disconnected.
- Your profile, sign-in, preferences and devices stay yours.

If you own a household that other people belong to, you can't join another one. Remove them first, or have them leave.

## After joining

You have your own login, and you see the household's shared data according to your role. See [What is a household](/en/docs/household/what-is-a-household#roles) for what each role can do.
