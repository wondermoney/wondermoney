---
title: Create a household
description: Learn how a household is created at signup and how other people join it.
---

A household is created at signup, not added later. When the first person signs up and subscribes, WonderMoney creates a household around that account and makes that person the owner.

Every account belongs to a household, even when it is the only person in it. To share your finances, invite the others to your household; to join someone else's, accept their invite.

## Creating at signup

The household is part of the first signup flow. The first person to sign up becomes the owner. After that, the owner can invite other people to join, and admins can too.

The owner role stays with the person who created the household until they transfer ownership to someone else. See [Transferring ownership](/en/docs/household/what-is-a-household#transferring-ownership).

## Joining with an existing account

Someone who already has an account on this server can accept an invite with it, as long as their current household has no accounts or transactions. Nothing from their old household is merged or moved. See [Join a household](/en/docs/household/join-a-household#joining-with-an-account-you-already-have).
