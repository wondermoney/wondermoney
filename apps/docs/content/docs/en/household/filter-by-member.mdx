---
title: Filter by member
description: See who spent what, mark joint accounts, and filter transactions, reports, budgets and net worth by household member.
---

In a household with more than one member, WonderMoney attributes every account and transaction to a member or to the household as a whole (**Shared**). Attribution answers "who spent this?". It never changes who can see the money: that is still [account privacy](/en/docs/household/account-privacy).

In a household with only you, none of this appears.

## Who an account and a transaction belong to

- An account belongs to its owner, the person who added or connected it.
- A **joint account** belongs to the household. Its spending counts as Shared.
- A transaction follows its account: its owner's, or Shared on a joint account.
- **Spent by** on a transaction overrides that: pick another member, or Shared. Choose **Account default** to follow the account again.
- Money attributed to someone who has left the household counts as Shared.

Existing accounts and transactions start with these defaults: every account belongs to its owner, no account is joint, and every transaction follows its account. Nothing changes until someone marks an account joint or picks **Spent by**.

## Mark an account as joint

Open the account's settings and turn on **Joint account**. Only the account's owner can change it. A joint account stays visible to the same people as before.

## Change who spent a transaction

Open the transaction and choose **Spent by**. Anyone in the household who can edit transactions can change it; viewers can't. The change is recorded in the transaction's history.

## Filter by member

The transactions list, reports, budgets, the dashboard and net worth have a member filter: **Everyone**, one member, or **Shared**.

- The totals for each member and for Shared add up to the household's total.
- A member's net worth counts the accounts, portfolios and manually valued assets they own. Joint accounts count under Shared. Loans count only in your own net worth. An account split by [ownership shares](/en/docs/household/ownership-shares) counts each member's percentage instead, and nothing under Shared.
- Filtering by another member never shows their private accounts, unless they shared one with you. You only ever see the accounts you could already see.
- On the budgets page the filter narrows what each budget counts, not which budgets are listed. Your [personal budgets](/en/docs/budgets/create-first-budget#household-or-personal-budget) stay yours: filtered by another member, a personal budget shows what that member spent among the transactions you can see, and it never appears for anyone else, whoever they filter by.

## Export and restore

A data export keeps which accounts are joint and every **Spent by** choice, and a restore brings them back. A restore merges everyone into the person restoring, so money another member spent stays attributed to a person, not to Shared.
