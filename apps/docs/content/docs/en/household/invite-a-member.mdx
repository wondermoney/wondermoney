The owner and admins invite people from the household settings. The invite goes to the person's email address with a link to join, and it stays pending until they accept it.

Members and viewers do not see the invite form.

## Sending an invite

1. Open the household settings.
2. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
3. Pick their role: admin, member or viewer. The owner can invite with any of the three; an admin can invite only as member or viewer. The role starts as member.
4. Send the invite.

The person can be new to WonderMoney or already have an account on this server. Either way, they join with the email address you invited. See [Join a household](/en/docs/household/join-a-household).

You can't invite yourself or someone who is already in the household. When the household is full, the invite is refused; see [How many people](/en/docs/household/what-is-a-household#how-many-people).

## Pending invites

<DocsImage
  src="/images/docs/household/invite-a-member-pending.png"
  alt="Pending invitations list with the invited email address, its role, its expiry date, and Resend and Revoke buttons."
  caption="An invite stays pending until the person accepts it."
  width={698}
  height={98}
/>

Every pending invite is listed in the household settings with its email address, role and expiry date. Everyone in the household can see them; the owner and admins can act on them:

- **Resend** sends a new email with a new link and gives the invite another 7 days. The old link stops working.
- **Revoke** cancels the invite, and its link stops working.

An admin can resend and revoke only invites for members and viewers. An invite expires 7 days after it was sent, and a pending invite holds a seat in the household until it is accepted, revoked or expires.

Share the link only with the person you invited. It works only for the invited email address.
