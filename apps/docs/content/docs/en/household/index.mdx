A household is a group of people who manage their money together in WonderMoney. Each person has their own login, and the household holds the shared data: accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, portfolio, cash flow, and net worth.

Use this section to understand how households are created, what each role can do, how people are invited and join, how account privacy works, and what happens when someone leaves.

## What you can do here

- Understand what a household is and when to use one.
- See what the owner, admin, member and viewer roles can do.
- Learn how a household is created at signup.
- Invite someone by email and choose their role.
- Join a household when you receive an invite, with a new account or one you already have.
- Control which accounts are shared with everyone, and who can view or edit the private ones.
- See who spent what, mark joint accounts, and filter by member.
- Split an account between members by percentage for each member's net worth.
- See how shared data appears in each member's view.
- Leave a household, or remove someone from it.

## How it works

A household is the unit that holds the data. One household has one or more members, up to 6 by default, each with their own login and a role. The person who creates the household becomes the owner. Other people join through an invite sent by email, either by signing up from the link or by accepting it with an account they already have.

Data in the household is shared by default. An account can be shared with the household or private, and a private account can be shared with chosen members, who can view it or edit it. Each member sees the accounts they are allowed to see.
