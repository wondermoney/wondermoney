Every account in a household has a privacy setting. A shared account is visible to everyone in the household; a private account is visible only to the person who owns it, whatever the other people's roles.

By default, an account is shared with the household. Change the setting on the account when you want it to stay private.

## Setting account privacy

Open the account and choose its privacy setting:

- **Shared** — everyone in the household sees the account, its transactions, and its balance. Viewers see it but can't change it.
- **Private** — only the person who owns the account sees it, including when the others are the owner or admins. Everyone else's views, including net worth, exclude the account.

Private accounts keep a person's personal spending out of the shared view, while shared accounts stay part of the household's common picture.

## Recurring series privacy

The owner can also set privacy for recurring series. A private recurring series is visible only to the person who owns it; a shared one appears in everyone's view. This works separately from the account-level setting.

## Privacy by data type

Privacy applies per data type. Accounts, transactions, categories, goals, budgets, portfolio, cash flow, and net worth follow the same household rules, and the owner decides which of these stay visible to everyone and which are limited to one person.
