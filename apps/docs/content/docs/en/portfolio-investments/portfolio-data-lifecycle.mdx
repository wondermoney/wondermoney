After you add or import transactions, a portfolio does not update all at once. Holdings and allocation are ready immediately; the performance history is built in the background from historical prices, and can take longer.

## What happens after a change

1. Your manual transaction or confirmed import is saved.
2. Holdings, their current value and the allocation update from the saved transactions.
3. WonderMoney fetches the historical prices of each asset you bought, from your first buy, up to ten years back.
4. The day-by-day history behind the performance chart is rebuilt for the dates the change affects.
5. The benchmark comparison and the risk metrics follow once that history exists.

Current prices refresh on weekdays.

## The status on a portfolio

While the history is not complete, the portfolio page on the web shows a notice with four lines: **Current holdings & value**, **Allocation**, **Performance history** and **Benchmark & risk metrics**, each with its own state.

| Notice | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Building performance history** | Historical prices are still being fetched for some assets. Holdings and allocation are already **Ready**; performance shows **Building** and the benchmark **Waiting** | Nothing. It finishes by itself |
| **Portfolio history is partially available** | The prices are in, and the history does not yet cover the whole period. Performance and the benchmark show **Partial** | Nothing. Charts and metrics may cover a shorter period for now |
| **Historical prices unavailable** | Some assets still lack historical prices more than three days after their latest buy was recorded, usually because the market-data provider has no older prices for them. All four lines read **Unavailable**, although holdings and allocation are still shown and current | Check that each asset was matched to the right ticker. Holdings stay up to date either way |

When there is no notice, the history is complete, or the portfolio has no buys yet.

The mobile app does not show this notice. While the history has fewer than two daily values, its performance chart shows **Not enough history yet to chart performance** instead of the chart, or **No performance history is available yet** when there is none. The advanced metrics carry a note when the performance history or the risk metrics need more history.

## What to expect

- Holdings can appear before the performance chart has any history.
- A metric that needs more history than exists shows as unavailable until the history is built.
- A missing history is not always an error. It often means the prices are still being fetched.
- A CSV import can be confirmed only once every row that needs asset review is resolved or its group is skipped. Rows in a skipped group are not imported. See [Review before anything is saved](/en/docs/portfolio-investments/import-portfolio-csv#review-before-anything-is-saved).
