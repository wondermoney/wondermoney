Holdings allocation shows how your portfolio is distributed across assets, asset classes, or other grouping rules the portfolio page supports.

## What to look for

- The largest positions in the portfolio.
- Concentration in a single asset.
- Whether cash is taking a large share of the portfolio.
- How allocation changes after new buys or sells.

## How to use it

- Review allocation after importing new holdings.
- Compare the distribution against your intended strategy.
- Use it to understand concentration, not to make a decision for you.
- [Allocation](/en/docs/glossary#allocation) explains how a portfolio is divided.
- [Holdings allocation metrics](/en/docs/portfolio-investments/portfolio-metrics-explained) explain how holding weight fits into total value and return.

## Common mistakes

- Reading allocation as advice.
- Forgetting that missing holdings can distort the chart or table.
- Comparing portfolios with different goals as if they should look identical.
