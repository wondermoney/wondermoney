Create a portfolio when you want to group a set of holdings, transactions, and performance into one investment view.

## When to create one

- You use more than one broker or account.
- You want to separate long-term investing from other holdings.
- You manage different strategies and want separate performance views.

## How to create it

1. Open Portfolio, under Investments in the sidebar.
2. Choose Create portfolio.
3. Enter a name that matches the account or strategy.
4. Pick the currency used by the portfolio.
5. Choose Create, then add holdings or import transactions.

## Good naming patterns

- Broker name plus account type, such as `DEGIRO Long Term`.
- Strategy name, such as `ETF Core`.
- A clear purpose, such as `Retirement` or `Kids savings`.

## Leave a portfolio out of the combined view

The Portfolios page adds up every portfolio in the household. To keep one out of it, such as money you hold for someone else:

1. Open the portfolio and choose **Portfolio settings**.
2. Turn on **Exclude from aggregate dashboard** and save.

The Portfolios page then leaves the portfolio out of its totals and gains, the holdings, Allocation, the metrics, Performance, Top Movers, the calendar and Dividend Income. It stays in the Portfolio Breakdown, marked **Excluded from totals**, so you can still open it. The setting applies to the whole household, and the [member filter](/en/docs/household/filter-by-member) leaves the portfolio out too.

The portfolio's own page is unchanged, and its daily history is still recorded. Net worth still counts the portfolio: the setting changes only the combined portfolio view. Turn it off to count the portfolio again.

## Common mistakes

- Reusing one portfolio for unrelated goals.
- Choosing the wrong currency.
- Waiting to add data until the portfolio is fully complete. You can create it first and fill it later.
