---
title: Link a Portfolio to Its Account
description: Stop net worth counting the same investments twice when a portfolio is held in one of your accounts.
---

A brokerage or investment account's balance often already includes the securities it holds. If you also track those securities as a portfolio, net worth would count them twice. Linking the portfolio to its account tells WonderMoney how to count that money once.

## Link a portfolio

1. Open the portfolio and choose Portfolio settings.
2. Under Account that holds this portfolio, choose the account.
3. Answer "Does this account's balance include your investments?".
4. Save.

You can also link a portfolio when you create it, or from the "Is this the same money?" suggestion on the home screen.

## Which answer to choose

| Answer | When it fits | How net worth counts it |
|---|---|---|
| Yes, it includes them | The account balance is the cash plus the value of your securities. | The account counts. The portfolio is listed under the account it is counted in and adds nothing to the total. |
| No, cash only | The account balance is only the uninvested cash. | The account's cash and the portfolio both count. |

WonderMoney preselects an answer when you choose the account: "No, cash only" for an account synced from your bank, because bank connections usually report only the cash, and "Yes" for an account you added by hand. Check your statement and change it if it does not match.

## What changes

- **Net worth today:** a portfolio inside its account's balance shows as "Counted in" that account, and the totals leave it out.
- **Net worth history:** the same rule applies at every point in the history and in the net worth history report. The current link is used for the whole history. Before the account existed, the portfolio counts on its own.
- **Unlinked portfolios** count as they always have.

## The "Is this the same money?" suggestion

When you can see an investment account that no portfolio is linked to, and your household has a portfolio that is not linked to an account, the home screen asks whether they are the same money. The Create portfolio dialog asks the same when such an account exists. Once an account and a portfolio are linked, by you or someone else in your household, the suggestion leaves them out. WonderMoney does not compare amounts; it only notices that both exist. Link them, or dismiss the suggestion if they are separate.

## Private accounts in a household

Portfolios are shared with your household; an account can be private, visible only to its owner and the members it is shared with. If you link a portfolio to your private account:

- In your net worth, the account counts and the portfolio is listed as counted in it.
- The members who can't see your account get the portfolio counted on its own in their net worth, and those it is shared with see it counted in the account, as you do. Each of you sees the money counted once.
- The members who can't see your account do not see the link and cannot change or remove it. If they try, WonderMoney tells them it is already linked by you, by name, so they know who to ask. It never shows them your account.

The other members can only link a portfolio to an account they can see.
