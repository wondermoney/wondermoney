---
title: Shared expense groups
description: Share costs with friends, flatmates or a trip, see who owes whom, and record when people pay each other back.
---

A group is a set of people who share costs: a trip, a flat, a dinner club. Share a transaction with the group and WonderMoney splits it into each person's share, works out who owes whom, and counts only your household's own shares in your spending.

## Before you start

- People in a group do not need a WonderMoney account. Add them by name; an email is optional and only kept for your reference. WonderMoney never writes to it.
- You can also add people from your household. Their shares count in the household's totals like yours.
- A shared cost is one of your transactions: the money left one of your household's accounts. The person who paid is always someone from your household.
- Sharing a transaction, or linking one to a settlement, changes what it counts, so you need to be able to edit its account. On an account shared with you to view only, you see its shared costs but cannot change them.

## Create a group

1. Open **Groups**. On the web it is in the main navigation; in the mobile app, open **More** and choose **Groups**.
2. Choose **New group**, give it a name and a currency, and add the people. You are added automatically.
3. Use **Add person** later to add more. Someone with shares, payments or settlements in the group cannot be removed.

## Split a transaction with a group

1. Open the transaction. On the web, choose **Split with group** in the edit sheet; in the mobile app, tap the transaction in the list to expand it and choose **Split with group**.
2. Choose the group and who paid.
3. Choose how to split it, and who is in it:
   - **Equally**: the amount is divided between the people you pick, to the cent. When it does not divide exactly, the first person takes the extra cent.
   - **By amount**: enter each person's part. The parts must add up to the transaction's amount.
   - **By percentage**: enter each person's percentage. They must add up to 100.
4. Optionally choose the category for the shares. By default they keep the transaction's category.

Each person's share becomes a line of the transaction's split. Choose **Edit shares** to change them, or **Stop sharing** to make the transaction whole again in one category. A shared transaction's split is edited from the group, not with the usual split editor.

## What counts in your spending

Only your household's shares count. A 60 dinner you paid and split equally between you and two friends counts 20 in Restaurants, in budgets, reports, the home page and the totals above the transactions list. The other 40 is money your friends owe you, so it is not your spending. Account balances do not change: the 60 really left your account.

When someone from your household is in the group too, their share counts in the household's totals as well, once.

The people in a group are not who **Spent by** names. A shared transaction keeps its one **Spent by** (its account's owner unless someone changed it), and the [member filter](/en/docs/household/filter-by-member) shows or hides it whole, at what it counts for the household: your household's shares together. If you and your partner each had 20 of a 60 dinner you paid, filtering by you shows 40, and filtering by your partner shows none of it. Pick **Spent by** on the transaction to file it under someone else.

## Balances

Each group shows a balance per person: what they paid, less their share, plus what they paid back, less what they were paid back. A positive balance means the group owes them; a negative one means they owe the group. A group's balances always add up to zero.

- Balances are kept per currency and never converted. A trip with costs in two currencies shows two sets of balances.
- **Suggested payments** lists the payments that would settle everyone up, with as few payments as WonderMoney can find.
- A cost on someone's private account is only in the balances that person sees, like the account itself.

## Record a settlement

When someone pays someone back, choose **Record settlement**, or pick one of the suggested payments. Enter who paid, who was paid, the amount and the day.

If the money went through one of your accounts, link the transaction: a friend's transfer into your account, or your payment to a friend. WonderMoney suggests matching transactions from the last 120 days, in the settlement's currency. A linked transaction no longer counts as income or spending for the linked amount, so a friend paying you back 20 is not 20 of income.

- Only a payment between someone of your household and someone outside it can be linked.
- A transaction can only play one part: a shared cost, a settlement, or a [reimbursement](/en/docs/accounts-transactions/understand-transactions#reimbursements). A friend paying back their share of a shared dinner is a settlement, not a reimbursement.
- Deleting a linked transaction leaves the settlement in place, unlinked.

## Archive or delete a group

Choose **Archive** when a trip is over: the group keeps its history and balances, and is no longer offered when you split a transaction. Only a group with no shared costs and no settlements can be deleted.

## Household roles

Everyone in your household sees the household's groups. A viewer can look at them but not change them.

## Common issues

- **I can't split a transfer or a loan repayment.** Transfers between your own accounts and loan repayments cannot be shared.
- **I can't mark a payment as a reimbursement.** It is shared in a group or settles a group debt; record it as a settlement instead.
- **Someone else paid.** For now, a shared cost is one of your household's transactions. Record what you pay that person back as a settlement.
