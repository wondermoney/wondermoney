Transactions are the record of money moving in or out of an account. Use the list to review imported activity, spot missing items, and keep categories current.

## Transaction lifecycle

A transaction moves through a defined lifecycle:

- **Pending.** The bank has reported the payment but has not confirmed it yet. Card payments usually start here.
- **Posted.** The bank has confirmed the transaction. A pending payment is matched to its posted version automatically, so the two do not stay separate for long.
- **Expired.** A pending transaction that the bank never confirms within the configured window is marked as expired and removed from the active list. This stops old pending records from lingering.

The available balance is the current balance net of pending activity, so money that is still pending is reflected in what you see.

## Audit log

Every change to a transaction — including duplicate handling and status changes — is recorded in an audit log. If you need to understand why a transaction is in a particular state, the audit history has the record.

## Attachments

Attach the paperwork for a purchase — a receipt, an invoice, a warranty — to its transaction. Open the transaction and use **Attachments** to drag files in or choose them; in the mobile app, take a photo or pick files from the device. Select a file to open it, and remove one with its delete control.

Each transaction holds up to 10 files of up to 10 MB each: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or PDF. The server checks the file's own bytes, so renaming a file does not change what it is. Attachments are private: download links are short-lived and only your household can see them. When you scan a receipt to create a transaction, the image is attached automatically.

Attachments need file storage on the server. If your instance has none, the section stays hidden.

## Change several transactions at once

Select transactions to categorize them, mark them as reviewed, hide or unhide them, add or remove a tag, or delete them in one step.

- **On the web**, tick the rows in the transactions list; the checkbox in the header selects every row on the page. A bar with the actions appears below the list.
- **In the mobile app**, open a row's menu and choose **Select**, then tick more rows, or choose **Select all loaded transactions**.

When every row on the page (in the mobile app, every loaded row) is selected and the date range and filters match more, choose **Select all matching transactions**. The action then covers every transaction the list matches, including the ones not loaded yet, and nothing outside it: never another household's transactions, nor private accounts you can't see. In the mobile app it is not offered while a filter that only narrows the loaded results (merchant, account, category or type) is on.

One action covers at most 5,000 transactions. If the filters match more, narrow them first.

## Delete transactions

You can delete transactions you added by hand, imported from a file or created from a receipt.

- **On the web**, open a transaction's menu and choose **Delete**, or select several and choose **Delete** in the bar.
- **In the mobile app**, open a row's menu and choose **Delete**, or select several and choose **Delete**.

WonderMoney asks first and says how many transactions will be deleted. A deleted transaction leaves every list, total and report, and the account's running balances are recalculated without it: the balance after each later transaction, and the balance of an account you keep by hand, are what they would have been had it never been entered. The audit log keeps a record of the deletion, but a deleted transaction cannot be restored.

Transactions your bank sent cannot be deleted, because the next sync would send them again. When a selection includes some, they are skipped and WonderMoney offers to hide them instead. A hidden transaction is left out of lists and totals; turn on **Show hidden transactions** in the filters to see it again.

## Merchant logos

A transaction with a merchant shows a small circle beside it in the list, the review inbox and the transaction's details. With no logo, the circle holds the merchant's first letter, and its color comes from the name, so the same merchant always has the same color.

To give a merchant a logo on the web, open one of its transactions, and under **Merchant** choose **Edit merchant details**:

- **Website**: the merchant's own site, such as `https://example.com`. If your instance has merchant logos turned on, WonderMoney fetches the logo for that site itself and shows it to you; the logo service never learns who you are. On an instance without them, the website is only stored.
- **Logo URL**: the address of an image to use instead. It must start with `https://`. Everyone in your household who sees the merchant loads that image from its own address, so only use one from a site you trust.

Leave a field empty to remove it. The merchant's details are shared with your household, and anyone in it but a viewer can change them. The mobile app shows the same logos and initials in its lists. When an image cannot be loaded, the first letter is shown instead.

## Before you start

Make sure the account has finished its first sync or has manual entries available. If you are reviewing a budget month, confirm the date range first.

## Steps

1. Open the transactions list for an account.
2. Filter by date, category, or account if needed.
3. Open a transaction to review the amount, merchant, and notes.
4. Update the category, description, or tags when required.

## Dates and date ranges

A date range covers whole days in your time zone. Banks usually send a booking date with no time, and WonderMoney counts such a transaction on that date wherever you are: a range that starts or ends on that day includes it, and a range that ends the day before does not. A transaction with a time, such as one you add yourself, counts at that time in your time zone. Lists and transaction details, on the web and in the mobile app, show a booking date as that same date, and a transaction with a time on its day in your time zone.

The accounts page's periods follow your calendar too: **1 Month** starts on the same day of the previous month, and **Year to Date** on 1 January, in your time zone. On recurring payments, **Last paid** and **Next expected** show a booking date as that same date, and a payment expected on a booking date stays under **Upcoming** for the whole of that day.

## Totals

Every total WonderMoney shows for a period adds up transactions you can list, and each one says which:

- **Money in** and **Money out** above the transactions list add up every transaction the list holds for the date range, on every account and whatever the table's filters, in your currency: other members' transactions are converted at the exchange rate of their date.
- **Reports** count posted transactions only, unless you turn on **Include pending transactions**, and leave out transfers between your own accounts, unless you turn on **Include transfers**.
- **The home page's** cash flow and spending by category count pending transactions too, and never transfers between your own accounts.

Hidden and expired transactions, and the parts of a transaction in a category that does not count as spending, are never in a report or home page total.

On the spending by category report, select the total to open the transactions list with exactly the transactions behind it: the same dates, filters and switches, money out only. The list shows what they add up to, which is the report's total. Your own filters on the list narrow them further, and **Show all transactions** goes back to the whole list.

## Reimbursements

A refund, or a friend paying you back, arrives as money in, while the purchase it covers still counts in full. Link the two and WonderMoney counts only what you really spent: the linked amount comes off the expense's category, and off your income.

- **On the web**, open the incoming transaction and choose **Mark as reimbursement**. **In the mobile app**, open it and choose **Mark as reimbursement**.
- Choose the expense it pays back. WonderMoney suggests expenses from the 60 days before it, from the same merchant or for at least its amount; search by description or merchant to look further back.
- Enter the amount to link. It starts at what both transactions still have free; a refund can cover part of an expense.

For example, a 20 refund linked to a 50 purchase in Groceries leaves 30 spent in Groceries, and 20 less income. Budgets, reports, the home page and the totals above the transactions list all count it this way. Account balances do not change: the money really moved.

- An expense can have several reimbursements, and one reimbursement can cover several expenses. What is linked never adds up to more than either transaction.
- When the expense is split, the linked amount comes off each line in proportion.
- The expense shows **Reimbursed** and the refund **Reimbursement**. Select the badge to open the other transaction. Open either to see its links, and choose **Unlink** to remove one; the totals go back to what they were.
- Deleting either transaction removes its links.
- For now, both transactions must be in the same currency. A transaction on a private account can only be linked to one on an account that exactly the same people can see, so nobody in the household sees half of a link. You also need to be able to edit both accounts.
- A transaction shared in an [expense group](/en/docs/accounts-transactions/shared-expense-groups), or one that settles a group debt, cannot also be a reimbursement: your friends' part is already left out of your totals.

## Typing amounts

Amount fields read numbers the way your app language writes them. In Portuguese and Spanish, the comma is the decimal separator and the dot groups thousands: `1.234,56` is one thousand two hundred thirty-four and 56 cents, and `1.000` is one thousand. In English it is the other way round: `1,234.56`, and `1.000` is one.

- A number with both separators, such as `1.234,56` or `1,234.56`, is read the same way in every language.
- Spaces and apostrophes can group thousands (`1 234,56`, `1'234.56`).
- A leading minus sign, parentheses, and a currency symbol or code (`-12,30 €`, `(12.30)`, `EUR 12,30`) are accepted.
- A value WonderMoney cannot read with certainty, such as `1.2.3` or `12,`, shows an error under the field instead of being saved as a different amount.

## Review

Look for uncategorized items, duplicate entries, and transactions that belong to a recurring payment. A short review each week keeps the account history usable for budgets and summaries.

## Common issues

- A pending card payment has not yet been matched to its posted version.
- A pending transaction shows as expired because the bank never confirmed it.
- Imported data is missing a recent payment.
- A manual correction was entered twice.
- The date range hides the transaction you are looking for.
