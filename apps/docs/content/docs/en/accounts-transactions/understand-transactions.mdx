Transactions are the record of money moving in or out of an account. Use the list to review imported activity, spot missing items, and keep categories current.

## Transaction lifecycle

A transaction moves through a defined lifecycle:

- **Pending.** The bank has reported the payment but has not confirmed it yet. Card payments usually start here.
- **Posted.** The bank has confirmed the transaction. A pending payment is matched to its posted version automatically, so the two do not stay separate for long.
- **Expired.** A pending transaction that the bank never confirms within the configured window is marked as expired and removed from the active list. This stops old pending records from lingering.

The available balance is the current balance net of pending activity, so money that is still pending is reflected in what you see.

## Audit log

Every change to a transaction — including duplicate handling and status changes — is recorded in an audit log. If you need to understand why a transaction is in a particular state, the audit history has the record.

## Attachments

Attach the paperwork for a purchase — a receipt, an invoice, a warranty — to its transaction. Open the transaction and use **Attachments** to drag files in or choose them; in the mobile app, take a photo or pick files from the device. Select a file to open it, and remove one with its delete control.

Each transaction holds up to 10 files of up to 10 MB each: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or PDF. The server checks the file's own bytes, so renaming a file does not change what it is. Attachments are private: download links are short-lived and only your household can see them. When you scan a receipt to create a transaction, the image is attached automatically.

Attachments need file storage on the server. If your instance has none, the section stays hidden.

## Before you start

Make sure the account has finished its first sync or has manual entries available. If you are reviewing a budget month, confirm the date range first.

## Steps

1. Open the transactions list for an account.
2. Filter by date, category, or account if needed.
3. Open a transaction to review the amount, merchant, and notes.
4. Update the category, description, or recurring status when required.

## Dates and date ranges

A date range covers whole days in your time zone. Banks usually send a booking date with no time, and WonderMoney counts such a transaction on that date wherever you are: a range that starts or ends on that day includes it, and a range that ends the day before does not. A transaction with a time, such as one you add yourself, counts at that time in your time zone. Lists and transaction details, on the web and in the mobile app, show a booking date as that same date, and a transaction with a time on its day in your time zone.

The accounts page's periods follow your calendar too: **1 Month** starts on the same day of the previous month, and **Year to Date** on 1 January, in your time zone. On recurring payments, **Last paid** and **Next expected** show a booking date as that same date, and a payment expected on a booking date stays under **Upcoming** for the whole of that day.

## Totals

Every total WonderMoney shows for a period adds up transactions you can list, and each one says which:

- **Money in** and **Money out** above the transactions list add up every transaction the list holds for the date range, on every account and whatever the table's filters, in your currency: the other member's transactions are converted at the exchange rate of their date.
- **Reports** count posted transactions only, unless you turn on **Include pending transactions**, and leave out transfers between your own accounts, unless you turn on **Include transfers**.
- **The home page's** cash flow and spending by category count pending transactions too, and never transfers between your own accounts.

Hidden and expired transactions, and the parts of a transaction in a category that does not count as spending, are never in a report or home page total.

On the spending by category report, select the total to open the transactions list with exactly the transactions behind it: the same dates, filters and switches, money out only. The list shows what they add up to, which is the report's total. Your own filters on the list narrow them further, and **Show all transactions** goes back to the whole list.

## Typing amounts

Amount fields read numbers the way your app language writes them. In Portuguese and Spanish, the comma is the decimal separator and the dot groups thousands: `1.234,56` is one thousand two hundred thirty-four and 56 cents, and `1.000` is one thousand. In English it is the other way round: `1,234.56`, and `1.000` is one.

- A number with both separators, such as `1.234,56` or `1,234.56`, is read the same way in every language.
- Spaces and apostrophes can group thousands (`1 234,56`, `1'234.56`).
- A leading minus sign, parentheses, and a currency symbol or code (`-12,30 €`, `(12.30)`, `EUR 12,30`) are accepted.
- A value WonderMoney cannot read with certainty, such as `1.2.3` or `12,`, shows an error under the field instead of being saved as a different amount.

## Review

Look for uncategorized items, duplicate entries, and transactions that belong to a recurring payment. A short review each week keeps the account history usable for budgets and summaries.

## Common issues

- A pending card payment has not yet been matched to its posted version.
- A pending transaction shows as expired because the bank never confirmed it.
- Imported data is missing a recent payment.
- A manual correction was entered twice.
- The date range hides the transaction you are looking for.
