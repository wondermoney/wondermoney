Use a manual offline account when you want to track cash, loans, or any balance you update yourself. It keeps those balances in the same place as your connected bank accounts without a live bank connection.

## Before you start

Decide how often you will update the balance and whether you will add transactions one by one or only keep a current balance. Manual accounts work best when the update habit is clear.

## Steps

1. Open the account creation flow.
2. Choose a manual or offline account type.
3. Enter the account name, currency, and starting balance.
4. Save the account and update it when the balance changes.

## Review

Check the balance after the first save and compare it with your notes or statement. If you later connect the same account to a bank feed, review the entries before changing the source of truth.

The starting balance counts toward your net worth history from the day you create the account, even before you add any transactions.

## Accounts in another currency

An account can be in a different currency from yours. WonderMoney converts its balance and transactions into your currency with the European Central Bank's daily rates, which cover the main currencies. When no rate exists for the account's currency, the account keeps its own balance but is left out of net worth, reports and the money available to assign to goals, and those totals are marked incomplete. It is never counted as if the amounts were already in your currency. In your net worth history, the starting balance is converted at each day's rate, and a day with no rate is marked incomplete.

The converted balance follows the latest rate: each time new rates are published, the balance is converted again. Transactions converted with a rate more than a week old, or with no rate at all, are converted again as soon as a rate for their date is published.

[Currencies and exchange rates](/en/docs/accounts-transactions/currencies-exchange-rates) explains the labels on amounts that are estimated or not converted.

## Common issues

- The starting balance was entered in the wrong currency.
- The account name is too generic to recognize later.
- Manual entries were mixed with imported transactions.
- The balance was not updated after a cash withdrawal or repayment.
