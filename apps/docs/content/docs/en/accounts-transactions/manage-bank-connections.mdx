---
title: Manage bank connections
description: Review, refresh, or remove connected banks and accounts.
---

Bank connections can expire, need re-approval, or stop syncing after a bank changes its security requirements. Use this page to keep imported data current and remove sources you no longer want to use.

## Connection status

On the web, **Settings → Connections** lists each bank with its accounts, when its consent expires, and a status:

- **Linked** — the connection is active.
- **Created** or **Authorized** — the connection was started, but the approval at the bank has not finished.
- **Expires in N days** — the consent ends within 14 days. **Reconnect** appears on the row.
- **Expired** — the consent has ended and the connection needs re-approval.
- **Ended by your bank** — the bank withdrew access before the consent's end date.
- **Error** — the connection could not complete.

The mobile app lists the same connections under **More → Bank connections**, labelled **Connected**, **Processing**, **Expiring soon**, **Consent expired**, **Ended by bank** or **Error**, with the date the consent expires and the date the data is fresh as of.

SimpleFIN connections are listed separately, as **Connected**, **First sync running**, **Access revoked**, **Expired** or **Error**.

You only see status for your own connections, and status is scoped to your account rather than to the bank or provider as a whole.

## When a connection or a sync fails

If you start a connection from **Accounts** on the web and come back from the bank without one, for example because you cancelled the approval, the page shows **Bank connection could not be completed**. No bank data is added, and the notice offers **Retry bank connection**, **Add account manually** and **Import CSV or bank statement**. The notice does not name the bank.

A sync that fails on an existing connection is recorded in that connection's history. The mobile app, and the SimpleFIN list on the web, also show the reason under the connection, and when a bank has not updated for more than 2 days, the home page says so and names the bank.

**Sync history** on a connection lists the last 20 times WonderMoney tried to sync it, with the result of each attempt.

If the list of banks cannot be loaded, for example during an outage at the provider, the mobile app shows **Could not load banks** with **Retry**. On the web, the list stays empty: close the dialog and try again later.

## Before you start

Check whether the problem is limited to one account or affects the whole bank connection. If a re-authentication prompt appears, complete it before making other changes.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Connections** on the web, or **More → Bank connections** in the mobile app.
2. Review each connection's status and when its consent expires. Open **Sync history** to see how recent syncs went.
3. Select **Reconnect** on a connection whose consent is ending, has expired or was ended by the bank, and approve it again at the bank. An account the bank reports with the same IBAN keeps its history. A SimpleFIN connection whose access was revoked needs a new setup token instead.
4. Remove a connection only if you no longer want new imports from it. On the web, select the unsync icon in the **Actions** column and confirm with **Unsync**; in the mobile app, or for a SimpleFIN connection, select **Disconnect**. The data already imported stays. Unlinking is safe to retry: if the first attempt does not complete, doing it again finishes the job.

## Review

After updating a connection, confirm that new transactions appear and that balances match expectations. If multiple accounts are attached to the same bank, check each one separately.

## Common issues

- The bank changed its login or security flow.
- One account synced while another did not.
- On the web, a removed connection still appears until the list is loaded again.
- The bank connection is valid, but the account is [private](/en/docs/household/account-privacy), so other household members do not see it.
