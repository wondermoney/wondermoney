Use the bank connection flow when you want WonderMoney to import balances and transactions from a supported financial institution through Open Banking. This is the main path for accounts that should sync automatically.

During onboarding, once an Enable Banking key is available, you can connect a bank directly from the account step, so you do not need to visit this flow separately. This page describes the standalone flow, which works the same way.

Connections to European banks go through Enable Banking and are read-only. You connect with an Enable Banking key of your own, which you add once in **Settings → Bank Provider**; [Connect your first bank account](/en/docs/start-here/connect-first-bank-account) walks through it. A self-hosted server may share one key with your household, and then you can skip that step.

## Before you start

Confirm that the bank is supported and that you are ready to approve the connection in the bank's own authentication flow. Until an Enable Banking key is available, the **Linked** option shows **Add your Enable Banking key first**, with a link to the Bank Provider settings.

## Steps

1. Open **Accounts**, select **Add account** and choose **Linked**.
2. Choose your bank's **Country**, then pick the bank from the list.
3. Read what happens next and select **Continue to bank**. You leave WonderMoney and sign in at your bank.
4. Approve read-only access. If your bank asks which accounts to share, choose them there: WonderMoney adds every account the bank shares.
5. You return to WonderMoney automatically. Your accounts and recent transactions are imported in the background and appear in **Accounts** shortly.

In the mobile app, open **More → Bank connections**, select **Connect a bank**, pick the bank and finish the consent in your browser. The Enable Banking key itself is added on the web.

## Banks in the United States and Canada

On a self-hosted instance that turns SimpleFIN on, **Connect with SimpleFIN** appears in the **Add account** dialog and in **Settings → Connections** on the web, and under **Bank connections** in the mobile app. Paste a setup token from SimpleFIN Bridge and select **Connect**; your accounts appear after the first sync. See [SimpleFIN for US and Canadian banks](/en/docs/self-hosting/simplefin).

## Review

After the connection is created, check the imported balances and transactions for the accounts you selected. If you also use manual accounts, keep them separate from the synced ones.

## Common issues

- The bank requires a second approval step.
- The wrong accounts were included during selection.
- The connection created successfully but the sync is still pending.
- The account already exists as a manual account.
