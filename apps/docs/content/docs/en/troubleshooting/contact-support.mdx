## Symptoms

Use this page when you need help with an account, bank connection, or import issue.

You may need support if:

- A feature is not behaving as expected
- An error keeps returning after a retry
- You cannot find the setting you need
- Your account data looks wrong

## What to check

Before you reach out, check the basics:

- Refresh the page and try again
- Confirm you are signed in to the correct account
- Check whether the issue affects one item or the whole app
- Review the related help article for the feature

## What to try

Try these steps first:

1. Sign out and sign back in.
2. Repeat the action in a new browser tab.
3. Remove and re-add the bank connection if the issue is connection-related.
4. Re-import the file if the issue is import-related.
5. Take a screenshot of the error.

## When to contact support

Contact support when:

- You have already tried the standard troubleshooting steps
- The problem blocks an important task
- You suspect account data loss or duplication

You can reach WonderMoney support by email at [support@wonder.money](mailto:support@wonder.money).

Include these details in your message:

- The page or feature where the issue happened
- The time it happened
- The exact error text
- What you expected to happen
- Screenshots or file samples if relevant
