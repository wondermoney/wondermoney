Before the bank flow starts, WonderMoney explains what happens next, including the read-only access and where your credentials go.

## Symptoms

- The bank does not finish connecting.
- The connection fails after you approve access at the bank.
- Accounts do not appear after the connection step.
- Existing links stop refreshing.
- Your Enable Banking key won't save, or WonderMoney reports it as invalid.

## What to check

- Whether you've saved a working Enable Banking key in Settings → Bank Provider — a bank connection can't start without one, unless your server provides a shared key or you connect through SimpleFIN.
- Whether the bank currently supports the connection method you are using.
- Whether you completed every approval step at the bank.
- Whether the account is connected to the correct account.
- Whether the connection expired or was revoked. When every account stops answering, WonderMoney asks the bank whether your consent still holds. If the bank ended it early, the connection shows as ended by the bank and stops refreshing until you reconnect it. See Consent length and reconnecting below.
- Whether the bank is limiting refreshes. Banks may allow only a few automatic refreshes a day. When a bank asks WonderMoney to slow down, the next automatic refresh waits until the time the bank gives, or about a day if it gives none.

## What to try

1. Start the connection again from the bank connection page.
2. Sign in to the bank carefully and confirm every permission screen.
3. Wait a few minutes and refresh the accounts list.
4. Remove the connection and reconnect it if the process is stuck.

## Consent length and reconnecting

Banks give WonderMoney read-only access for a limited time. Under PSD2 a consent lasts at most 180 days, and each bank sets its own limit, often 90 or 180 days. The connections list in Settings shows when each consent ends.

- **Expiring soon.** In the last 14 days before a consent ends, the connection shows how many days are left and offers **Reconnect**, on the web and in the mobile app.
- **Expired.** The consent reached its end date. Data stops refreshing until you reconnect.
- **Ended by your bank.** The bank ended the consent before its end date, for example after you changed your online banking password or withdrew access in your banking app. Data stops refreshing until you reconnect.

WonderMoney also emails you about 7 days and again within the last day before a consent ends, and when the bank ends it early. See [Email preferences](/en/docs/settings-security-privacy/email-preferences) to turn these alerts off.

Reconnecting starts a new consent with the same bank. When it completes, WonderMoney matches the bank's accounts to the ones you already have by account number (IBAN) and currency, so your accounts, balances and transaction history stay as they are. The old connection leaves the list once all its accounts have moved to the new one. An account you removed is not brought back, and an account the bank shares without an account number may appear as a new account.

## Enable Banking key problems

Most connection problems trace back to how the Enable Banking application was set up, not to the bank itself. Before retrying a connection, check for these:

- **Application created with Sandbox instead of Production.** Sandbox only ever connects to fake, mock bank data, so it can never link a real account. Check the application's page in Enable Banking's Control Panel — if it shows a Sandbox environment, register a new application with **Production** selected instead. See [Connect your first bank account](/en/docs/start-here/connect-first-bank-account) for the full setup.
- **The application form wouldn't submit.** For a Production application, Enable Banking requires a Privacy URL and a Terms URL, but doesn't mark them as required or show an error when they're missing — the form just does nothing when you click submit. If an application won't save, check that both fields are filled in.
- **"Request activation" was clicked instead of "Activate by linking accounts."** Enable Banking's Control Panel shows both buttons after an application is registered. "Request activation" starts a business verification (KYB) process that isn't needed for personal use and won't activate a personal application on its own. Use "Activate by linking accounts" instead, which activates the application by walking you through linking one of your own bank accounts.

<Callout type="error" title="Lost the downloaded private key">
  Enable Banking shows the private key exactly once, at the moment it's generated, and cannot show it again — not even through their own support. If the `.pem` file wasn't backed up and can no longer be found, there is no way to recover it. The only fix is to register a brand-new application in the Control Panel and generate a new key.
</Callout>

## Sync errors and history

A failed sync is recorded in the connection's history. Select **Sync history** on the connection, on the web or in the mobile app, to see the last 20 times WonderMoney tried to sync it and how each one ended. **Error** in **Settings → Connections** means the connection itself could not be completed, not that a sync failed. WonderMoney syncs each connection automatically about once a day, and a failed sync may be retried once, half an hour later. A connection that keeps failing usually needs **Reconnect**.

During an outage at the provider, such as Enable Banking, the bank list cannot be loaded: the mobile app says so and offers **Retry**, and on the web the list stays empty. Your existing data stays unchanged. You can start the connection again once the provider is available.

For the full connection workflow, see [Manage bank connections](/en/docs/accounts-transactions/manage-bank-connections).

## When to contact support

Contact support if the bank is listed as supported but the connection still fails after a retry, or if the account connects but no data syncs after a reasonable wait.
