---
title: Wrong Balances
description: Rebuild an account's balances when its running balance or total looks wrong.
---

Every transaction in an account carries a running balance: what the account held right after it. If those figures stop adding up, **Recalculate balances** rebuilds them from scratch.

## Symptoms

- The running balance beside a transaction is not the one before it plus the transaction's amount.
- An account's balance does not match its opening balance plus its transactions.
- The balance history chart jumps on a day with no transaction.
- A foreign-currency account shows no converted balance, or one that no longer follows the exchange rate.

## What to check

- Whether a pending transaction is involved. Pending and expired transactions do not count towards the running balance until they settle.
- For a bank-connected account, whether the latest sync finished. Its balance is the one your bank reports.
- For a manual or imported account, whether the opening balance is right. It is the balance you entered when you created the account.

## What to try

1. On the web, open **Accounts**, open the menu beside the account and choose **Recalculate balances**. On mobile, open the account, tap the more-actions menu and choose **Recalculate balances**.
2. Confirm. The recalculation runs in the background, so you can close the window or leave the screen.
3. When it finishes, a message says how many transactions were corrected and shows the balance before and after. "Already correct" means nothing needed fixing.

What the recalculation does:

- A manual or imported account starts from its opening balance and adds each settled transaction in date order. The account's balance becomes the result.
- A bank-connected account works backwards from the balance your bank reports, which is never changed.
- Amounts that were stored without an exchange rate are converted again where a rate now exists, and the balance in your currency is refreshed at the latest rate.
- The transactions themselves (amounts, dates, descriptions, categories) are not changed, and on an account that is already right nothing changes, so it is safe to run more than once.

Any household member who can see the account can recalculate it; a private account can only be recalculated by its owner. Each account can be recalculated once a minute.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the balances are still wrong after a recalculation. If a transaction is missing or appears twice, see [Transaction import problems](/en/docs/troubleshooting/transaction-import-problems) first; for a bank-connected account whose bank balance itself is wrong, see [Bank connection problems](/en/docs/troubleshooting/bank-connection-problems).
