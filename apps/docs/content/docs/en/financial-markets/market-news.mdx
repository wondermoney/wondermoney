Market news collects recent articles and updates for assets you are researching or tracking.

Use it to add context to price moves, not to replace your own review of the asset.

## How news is presented

- Each item is a short text snippet with a headline and a limited amount of text.
- Links open the original article on the publisher's site.
- News items contain text only; images and embedded content are not shown.

## Why some links are plain text

Market news only links to sources with a validated HTTPS address. If an article does not pass that check, its link is not shown and the item appears as plain text. This is why some articles appear without a link even though the headline is visible.

## How to use it

- Open news from an asset page or the market news section.
- Scan headlines before reading the full article.
- Use news as context alongside price history and fundamentals.

If several articles are covering the same event, open the original source before relying on a summary.

## Common mistakes

- Reacting to a headline without checking the source.
- Treating news as a trade signal.
- Assuming all news items are equally relevant to the asset.
