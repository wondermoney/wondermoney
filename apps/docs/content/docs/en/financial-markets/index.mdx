Financial Markets is the research area for stocks, ETFs, and other listed instruments. It is available to all users.

Use it to search assets, review an asset's price and price history, and save items to a watchlist.

## What you can do here

- Search an asset by ticker, name, or keyword.
- Open an asset page to review its price, its day change and its price history.
- Save instruments to a watchlist that is stored on your account and syncs across devices.

## Data note

Market data is for research and tracking only. WonderMoney does not provide investment advice.

Fundamentals, financials, earnings and market news are not part of the market-data plan WonderMoney runs on today. The fundamentals, financials and earnings panels on an asset page say so; prices, price history and company profiles are unaffected.

When no live price is available for an asset, its page shows the last close and its date instead.

Market data services are rate-limited. When an upstream provider is unavailable, the affected views keep showing the last available figures rather than failing.

## Common mistakes

- Using a single data point to decide on a trade.
- Confusing watchlists with portfolios. A watchlist is research; a [portfolio](/en/docs/portfolio-investments) is a record of what you hold.
- Assuming all market figures update at exactly the same time.
