## How search works

- Search matches tickers, asset names, and keywords.
- On the web, an exact ticker match is ranked first, then listings on the home exchange, whose ticker has no exchange suffix.
- Results refresh as you type. The field waits briefly after you stop typing before it fetches new results: a fifth of a second on the web, about a third of a second in the mobile app.

## Search behaviour

- Use the keyboard to move through the results and press Enter to select one.
- When you focus the search field, any existing text is selected, so you can start typing and replace it cleanly instead of clearing it by hand.
- An empty search field is safe: no results are shown and no error is raised. Type a few characters to see matches.

## Search tips

- Search by ticker first if you know it.
- Try the asset name if the ticker is not obvious.
- Use broad keywords when you are exploring a market or sector.

## What the results are for

- Opening the asset detail page.
- Adding an asset to your watchlist.
- Checking the market data before importing it into a portfolio.

An asset's page shows its price, its day change and its price history. The fundamentals, financials and earnings panels currently say that their data is not part of the market-data plan WonderMoney runs on today.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing the wrong share class or exchange listing.
- Assuming similar names mean identical assets.
- Using search results as a buy or sell signal.
