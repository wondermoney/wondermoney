The watchlist is a saved list of assets you want to monitor without adding them to a portfolio yet.

It helps separate research from actual holdings.

## Stored on your account

The watchlist is stored against your WonderMoney account, not in your browser. This means the list is the same wherever you sign in: add an item on your phone and it appears on the web, and the other way around.

Because the list is tied to your account, it is not lost when you switch devices, browsers, or clear your browser data.

## What the change figure means

The watchlist shows a change figure for each asset. This is the day change — the difference between the latest price and the previous day's close. It is not a live or streaming price. The figure updates when market data refreshes, not continuously.

## Use it for

- Tracking instruments you may want to research later.
- Following a shortlist of assets across markets.
- Keeping a clean separation between research and holdings.

## What to expect

- Saved assets stay in one place across your devices.
- You can open each asset from the list to review its details.
- The watchlist does not change portfolio performance or holdings.

## Common mistakes

- Adding every interesting asset and losing focus.
- Using the watchlist as a record of owned investments.
- Expecting watchlist items to affect portfolio totals.
- Reading the day change as a real-time price.
