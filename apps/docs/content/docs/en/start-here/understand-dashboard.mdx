The dashboard, the **Home** page of the app, brings balances, recent activity, and shortcuts into one view. Use it to confirm that your accounts are connected and to reach transactions or budgets quickly.

## Before you start

Make sure at least one account or budget exists so the dashboard has real data to display. New accounts may show empty states until the first sync finishes. Completing the onboarding marks your first run complete, so the dashboard renders normally on the next sign-in.
If you are still learning the terms on this page, see the [glossary](/en/docs/glossary).

- **Net worth** for the overall position: what you own less what you owe, with its change over time. It is always shown first.
- **Insights** for spending that is above or below its usual pace, and budgets on course to overrun.
- **This month** for what you have spent against your budget, and what is left to spend.
- **Balance sheet** for what you own and what you owe, grouped into accounts, portfolios, property and other assets, and loans.
- **Net cash flow by month** for money in less money out, with income, spending, and savings rate.
- **Next loan payments** for the loan payments coming up.
- **Latest transactions** for the latest activity, and how many transactions are waiting for review.
- Sidebar navigation for accounts, loans, budgets, groups, portfolio, assets, goals, and reports. Settings are in the account menu.

Select **Customize** to hide sections or change their order. Until you have added an account, reviewed your transactions, created a budget, and created a portfolio, a checklist shows the steps that remain. When a bank connection is about to expire or has stopped updating, a notice at the top offers **Reconnect**.

## How to use it

Check net worth first, then review the latest transactions for anything unexpected. Use the links in each section to move into the area that needs attention, such as a missing import or an unassigned category.

## Common issues

- Balances are stale because a connection has not synced yet.
- A new account is still empty after signup.
- Recent activity does not include a manual account until you add entries.
