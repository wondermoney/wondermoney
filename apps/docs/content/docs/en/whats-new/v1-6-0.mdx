## Upgrading

Nothing to do. This release has no migrations and no new settings.

## New

- **PPR comparison:** every retirement savings plan (PPR) on sale in Portugal, from the CMVM and ASF lists, with its returns, costs and risk class, on wonder.money.
- **Resource lists:** podcasts, newsletters, YouTube channels, forums, books, courses and financial-education events, chosen for each market by published criteria and reviewed on a schedule.
- **Where signups come from:** when you allow analytics on both the website and the app, a signup from one of the website's free tools or lists, and your first budget and goal, are counted against that page. Nothing is stored with your account. See [Data Privacy](/en/docs/settings-security-privacy/data-privacy).

## Fixes

- The savings guide quotes the current tax on PPR redemptions.
