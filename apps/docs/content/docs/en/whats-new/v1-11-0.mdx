## Upgrading

Nothing to do. There are no migrations and no new settings, and the Docker Compose, Helm and Render installs work as before.

## New

- **Fuel prices near you.** The fuel prices page on wonder.money lists the cheapest stations within 5, 10 or 25 km of where you are, with the distance on each row. Your location stays in your browser. A map shows each station with its price, and each fuel type has its own colour.
- **Logos** beside every bank, electricity supplier and fuel brand on the website's free bank accounts, term deposits, electricity and fuel comparisons.
- Every page on wonder.money has its own preview image when you share it, and the homepage's net worth example shows how it changed over six months.

## Fixes

- The FAQ links on the Portuguese and Spanish website opened the English FAQ.
