## Upgrading

Nothing to do. This release has no migrations and no new settings.

## New

- **Fuel prices** is now in the Comparators menu on wonder.money, next to electricity.

## Fixes

- Calculator fields on wonder.money keep the value clear of its unit, however long the unit is in your language. Help and error lines under fields share one style.
- The learn and glossary searches on wonder.money have a name that screen readers announce.
- Plainer wording for settling up on the household page in Spanish and Italian.
