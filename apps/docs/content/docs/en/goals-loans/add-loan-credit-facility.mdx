Add a loan or credit facility when you want to track what you owe, the balance remaining, and the repayments you make over time.

## What to enter

- Name of the loan or credit facility, and its type: mortgage, auto, personal, student or other
- Principal, the balance on the start date, with its currency, and the start date
- Optionally the original loan amount, the monthly installment, the interest rate and its type, the term in months and the amortization method
- Optionally the asset the loan is secured against, and whether the loan counts in net worth
- Optional notes such as lender or purpose

## How it works

Loans are tracked as liabilities in WonderMoney. They do not change your bank account balance. The balance follows the repayments you link and the balance adjustments you record; see [Track repayments](/en/docs/goals-loans/track-repayments). The interest rate and term feed the schedule and the payoff projection; see [Loans overview](/en/docs/goals-loans/loans-overview).

## Common mistakes

- Entering a payment as a new loan.
- Using a goal instead of a liability entry.
- Expecting the loan record to reconcile itself to bank transfers.
