Create a goal when you want to set aside money for a specific purpose, such as an emergency fund, a trip, taxes, or a future purchase, or when you want to plan paying off a debt.

## Choose a goal type

When you create a goal, choose the type first:

- **Save up** reserves money toward a target amount, such as an emergency fund or a future purchase.
- **Pay off debt** creates a debt pay down goal, which tracks paying off one debt, such as a loan or a card.

The goal type is part of the goal setup: it can't be changed after the goal is created. Everything else, the target amount included, can be changed later when you edit the goal.

## Save up goals

For a save up goal, set:

- Goal name
- Target amount
- Target date, if you have one
- Monthly contribution target, if you want one

## Debt pay down goals

A debt pay down goal tracks one debt. Set:

- Goal name
- Current balance
- Interest rate (APR)
- Minimum monthly payment
- Extra monthly payment, if you pay more than the minimum
- Target payoff date, if you have one

If the debt is one of your loans on the Loans page, choose it under **Link a debt account (optional)** to fill in the name, balance, rate and payment from the loan.

The goal then shows its payoff plan: the payoff date, the time to payoff, the total interest and a timeline of the balance. When the monthly payment doesn't cover the interest, the goal says the payment is too low instead of showing a date.

With two or more debt pay down goals, the goals page shows **Debt payoff strategy**. It compares paying your debts in two orders, with the same total monthly budget:

- **Avalanche** — orders debts by interest rate, highest first. This usually minimizes the total interest paid.
- **Snowball** — orders debts by balance, smallest first. This usually clears the smallest balances sooner.

For each order you see the time to payoff and the total interest, and how much interest avalanche saves over snowball. It is a comparison only: you don't pick a strategy, and nothing is saved on the goals.

In the mobile app you can create save up goals; create a debt pay down goal on the web. Debt goals and the strategy comparison appear in both.

## Priority

Set the priority in the create or edit form. Goals with a higher priority appear first in goal lists. You can change the priority later. See [Goal priority](/en/docs/goals-loans/goal-priority) for details.

## Assigning money

The goal stores a target and tracks progress through goal activity. It does not move money between bank accounts. You can assign money to the goal manually and then fund an expense from it when needed.

When you assign money, you cannot assign more than **Available to assign**: the balance of your open accounts less the money already assigned to goals. A confirmation message appears when the assignment succeeds. **Move money out** takes money back out of a goal.

## Good goal names

- Emergency fund
- Annual taxes
- New laptop
- Vacation 2026

## Common mistakes

- Setting a target that does not match the real purpose of the money.
- Creating too many small goals that are hard to manage.
- Expecting the goal to automatically reserve money from a bank account.
- Choosing a debt pay down strategy without comparing the payoff timeline first.
