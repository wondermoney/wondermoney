# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This page summarizes how cancellation and refunds work for **WonderMoney** subscriptions, provided by **Radix Catch, Lda**. It supplements — and does not replace — the [Terms and Conditions](/about/terms-of-service), §6.

---

## 1. Free Trial

New accounts start with a **14-day full-access trial**, no credit card required. You can cancel or simply stop using the app at any point during the trial at no cost.

---

## 2. Billing Periods

After the trial, continued access requires an active subscription, billed either **monthly** or **annually**, at the price shown on the [pricing page](https://www.wonder.money/pricing) at the time of subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled.

---

## 3. Cancelling a Monthly Subscription

You can cancel a monthly subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current monthly billing cycle** — you keep access until then, and you are not billed again afterward. Monthly subscriptions are not eligible for a pro-rated refund of the current cycle.

---

## 4. Cancelling an Annual Subscription

- **Within 30 days of the initial annual subscription**: you can cancel for any reason, with a refund of **91.6% of the amount paid**.
- **After the initial 30 days**: you can cancel the renewal of your annual subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current annual cycle** — you keep access until then, with no refund for the remainder of the current cycle.

---

## 5. How to Cancel

Cancel anytime from within the app (Settings → Subscription), or contact [support@wonder.money](mailto:support@wonder.money) for assistance.

---

## 6. Refund Requests

Refund requests outside the cases described above (for example, billing errors) are reviewed case by case. Contact [support@wonder.money](mailto:support@wonder.money) with your account email and the reason for the request.

---

## 7. Changes to Pricing or Plans

Any changes to pricing or plan terms are communicated at least 30 days in advance by email, as described in the [Terms and Conditions](/about/terms-of-service), §6.3 and §7.

---

## 8. Governing Language

This document is published in English, Portuguese and Spanish. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the **English version** prevails.
