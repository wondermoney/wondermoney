## 4. Types of Data Collected

When using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

### Cookie and Tracking Data

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and retain certain information.

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that may include an anonymous unique identifier. Cookies are sent to your browser from a website and stored on your device. Other tracking technologies are also used, such as beacons, tags and scripts, to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you choose not to accept cookies, you may not be able to use some parts of our Service.

**Examples of cookies we use:**

- **Session cookies** - we use session cookies to operate our Service.
- **Preference cookies** - we use preference cookies to remember your preferences and various settings.
- **Security cookies** - we use security cookies for security purposes.
- **Analytics cookies** - analytics cookies may be used to understand how the Service is used and improve its reliability and usability.

## 12. Analytics

We may use third party Service Providers to monitor and analyze the use of our Service.

### Google Analytics

Google Analytics is a web analytics service provided by Google that monitors and reports on website traffic. It is delivered through Google Tag Manager, which loads on the marketing site and the web application. If used, it helps us understand how the Service is used and improve reliability and usability. WonderMoney does not use financial data for advertising and does not sell user data.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please visit: [https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)

We also encourage you to review Google's policy for safeguarding your data: [https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245)

### PostHog

PostHog is a product analytics platform used to understand product usage and improve the Service. It receives your email address and usage events, and it records sessions within the web application — meaning the screens you view, which can include your account balances and transaction descriptions. Session recording is disabled on pages where you submit feedback. PostHog processes this data in the European Union.

For more information about PostHog, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://posthog.com/privacy](https://posthog.com/privacy)

### Sentry

Sentry is an application performance monitoring platform that helps developers identify and fix errors in real time, with alert systems.

For more information about Sentry, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://sentry.io/privacy/](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

### Supabase

Supabase is the authentication and identity provider for WonderMoney. It processes your email address, your login credentials and your multi-factor authentication state, in the European Union. Your account data, transactions and balances are **not** stored by Supabase — they are held in our own database, on infrastructure we operate.

For more information about Supabase, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

### Vercel

Vercel hosts the WonderMoney web application. Hosting and edge processing are performed in Vercel's infrastructure, with primary regions in the European Union.

For more information about Vercel, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)

### Sanity

Sanity is the content management system used for marketing and editorial content on WonderMoney. Sanity stores public-facing editorial content (guides, glossary entries) and is hosted in the European Union.

For more information about Sanity, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://www.sanity.io/legal/privacy](https://www.sanity.io/legal/privacy)

### Resend

Resend is the transactional email provider used to send account emails (verification, password reset, important notifications). Resend processes email addresses and message content on our behalf.

For more information about Resend, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy)

### EODHD

EODHD is used to look up market prices for investment holdings. Only publicly available ticker symbols and aggregate price data are sent to EODHD — no personal data, account data, or transaction data.

For more information about EODHD, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/privacy-policy](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/privacy-policy)

### netcup

netcup GmbH provides the servers on which WonderMoney runs. Our application, our background processing and our database are hosted there, which means netcup's infrastructure holds your account data, your transactions and your balances. The servers are located in Nuremberg, Germany, within the European Union.

For more information about netcup, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://www.netcup.com/en/general-information/privacy-policy](https://www.netcup.com/en/general-information/privacy-policy)

### Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services provides the object storage where documents you upload are kept: bank statement files and portfolio files you import, and receipt or invoice images you submit for automatic reading. These are stored in the London (eu-west-2) region, in the United Kingdom. The United Kingdom is recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection.

For more information about Amazon Web Services, please visit their Privacy Notice: [https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/)

### Google reCAPTCHA

Google reCAPTCHA protects our signup and contact forms from automated abuse. It processes your IP address, browser and device characteristics, and how you interact with the page, in order to score whether a submission is likely to be automated. This runs on visitors to those forms, including people who never create an account.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please visit: [https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)

### LLM Models

Where LLM features are used, Radix Catch, Lda uses providers configured so customer data is not used to train public models, subject to the applicable provider terms.

The primary location of these services will be in Europe, and the user will be notified if data is processed outside this region.

### GoCardless, SAS

GoCardless, SAS is a licensed Account Information Service Provider that will act upon user instruction and indication to access and share bank account information, including bank transactions.

Access will not include sensitive payment data.

For more information about GoCardless, SAS, please visit their [Privacy Policy for bank account holders](https://gocardless.com/privacy/account-holders/).

### Enable Banking

New bank connections use Enable Banking, a Registered Account Information Service Provider regulated by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA). WonderMoney does not hold a shared connection with Enable Banking on your behalf: you register your own free Enable Banking application and provide its Application ID and private key, and our servers use those credentials to access and share your bank account information, including your transactions, on your instruction.

Your private key is encrypted at rest and is used only to sign requests to Enable Banking from our servers — it is never sent to Enable Banking itself. Access does not include the ability to move money or make payments.

For more information about Enable Banking, please visit their [Privacy Policy](https://enablebanking.com/privacy-policy).
