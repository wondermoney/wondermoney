## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your personal data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations (for example, if we are required to retain your data to comply with applicable laws), resolve disputes and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

We also retain Usage Data for internal analysis purposes. Usage Data is generally retained for a shorter period, except when this data is used to strengthen security or improve the functionality of our Service, or when we are legally required to retain this data for longer periods.

### Retention Periods

The periods below apply to the hosted Service. A self-hosted server keeps data as its operator configures it.

- **Your account.** We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time: on the web, in **Settings → Privacy → Delete WonderMoney account**; in the mobile app, in **More → Settings → Security → Delete account**. The deletion takes effect at once, with no grace period, and cannot be undone. At that moment we delete your sign-in record (email address, password hash, passkeys, two-factor authentication, sessions and the AI clients connected with OAuth), replace the name and email address in your profile with placeholder values and remove your phone number, disconnect your bank connections, delete your email notification preferences and the phones registered for notifications, and turn off the assistant. If you are a member of a household, the accounts and transactions you added stay with that household. If you own the household, it is dissolved and its other members lose access to it. The financial records of a dissolved household, and your anonymised account record, are erased 30 days after the deletion. Our support inbox receives a notice of the deletion with your email address. We delete that notice after 30 days.
- **Sign-in sessions.** A session records the IP address and browser of the device you signed in on. It ends after 7 days without use; using the Service extends it, at most once a day. It is deleted when you sign out, sign out of other devices or everywhere, or delete your account. Sessions of the public demo account end after 2 hours.
- **Personal API tokens.** A token is valid for the period you choose when you create it: 30, 90 or 365 days (90 days for a token created with **Create an AI client token**). A token you revoke stops working at once. Its record, without the token itself, is kept marked as revoked so that the assistant audit log can still name it.
- **AI clients connected with OAuth.** An access token is valid for 15 minutes and a refresh token for 30 days. Each refresh replaces the refresh token, so a client you keep using stays connected until you disconnect it. Disconnecting deletes your consent and the client's access tokens and revokes its refresh tokens at once. A client's registration, with its revoked tokens, is removed 7 days after its last connection ended, or 7 days after it registered if nobody connected it; a daily job removes it on the next run.
- **Pending Enable Banking keys.** The key we generate for your Enable Banking application waits 7 days to be activated. If you have not activated it by then, it is deleted on the next daily run. An activated key that you remove, or that belonged to a deleted account, is kept encrypted for 30 days and then deleted.
- **Assistant audit log.** The log of tool calls by the in-app assistant and by AI clients (see "Connected AI Clients" in section 8) is kept for 30 days.
- **Email and notification records.** We keep a record of each email we send you (its type, when it was sent and whether it was delivered) and of the bank sync alerts and reminders we have sent, so that the same message is not sent twice. These records are deleted after 30 days. A phone registered for notifications is removed when the session it was registered with ends. Resend, which delivers our email, keeps the messages it sends for 30 days.
- **Analytics (PostHog).** A service event that cannot be delivered to PostHog within 30 days is deleted, and events not yet sent are deleted at once when you turn off **Service analytics**. Our copy of a service event is deleted 30 days after it is sent. PostHog keeps the events it receives for 1 year.
- **Error reports (Sentry).** Sentry keeps error reports for up to 90 days.
- **Uploaded files and statement imports.** A bank statement you upload for import, and the lines read from it, are deleted 7 days after you complete the import; we keep a summary of the import (file name, status, dates and the number of rows). An import you do not complete expires after 7 days and is deleted with its file. A file you attach to a transaction is deleted from storage when you remove it. A data export is deleted 24 hours after it is created, and a backup file you upload to restore is deleted when the restore ends, at the latest a day after it started. A receipt or invoice image you submit for automatic reading, and a portfolio file you import, are deleted 30 days after they are processed.
- **Server logs.** Our servers log each request: its method, address and result and, for a request that fails, the network address it came from and the browser's user agent. We keep server logs for 30 days at most.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can remain in database backups until they expire. Database backups are kept for 30 days.
- **Billing records.** The Service is free, so we keep no billing records.

### Connected AI Clients

You can connect an AI client, such as ChatGPT, Claude or another client that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), to your account: by allowing it with OAuth, or by giving it a personal API token you create. We share nothing with an AI client unless you connect it yourself.

Once it is connected, when you ask the client something and it calls one of WonderMoney's read-only tools, we send it what that tool returns: your accounts with their names, types, currencies and balances; your net worth, with its assets, liabilities and portfolios; money in and out, spending by category and the monthly spending trend; individual transactions with their date, amount, description, account and category (at most 100 per request); budgets and how they are doing; categories; savings and debt goals and their progress; recurring payments due in the next 30 days; investment portfolios, their value and dividend income; and the insights the app shows. The client sees only what you see in the app: the household's shared data, your own private accounts and those shared with you, never a member's private accounts hidden from you and never another household's data. The tools cannot change anything. With a personal API token you may also let the client propose a category, a reviewed mark or a note for your transactions; each proposal waits in the app until you apply or dismiss it.

The client's provider (for example OpenAI for ChatGPT, or Anthropic for Claude) receives this data and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. It is not one of our Service Providers, and we do not control what it keeps. Read its privacy policy before you connect it.

We keep an audit log of every tool call an AI client makes: the tool's name, the personal API token or OAuth client it came through, the time, whether it succeeded, and summary details such as the period asked about and the number of records returned. Amounts, descriptions and other content are never written to it. The most recent calls are listed in **Settings → API tokens → Connect an AI client**.

To disconnect a client connected with OAuth, select **Disconnect** next to it under **Connected with OAuth** in **Settings → API tokens**. To stop a client that uses a personal API token, select **Revoke** next to the token in **Settings → API tokens**. Either way, its access ends at once. Changing or resetting your password, signing out of other devices or everywhere, and leaving your household also disconnect every client connected with OAuth. Section 6 sets out how long tokens last and when unused clients are removed.

### PostHog

PostHog is a product analytics platform used to understand product usage and improve the Service. It processes this data in the European Union and receives two kinds of events.

**Service events.** When you use the hosted Service, our servers send PostHog a short, fixed list of events about your account: that you signed up or signed in; that you used WonderMoney on a given day (at most once a day, saying only whether it was through the web app, the mobile app or the API); that you created an account, a budget or a goal; that you completed an import, with its file format; and how a bank connection or a bank sync went, with the provider and a fixed error category. They are keyed by a random identifier for your account, never your email address or name, and carry no amounts, balances, names, descriptions or other content you entered. We send them on the basis of our legitimate interest in knowing whether the Service works and which parts of it people use, so they do not depend on the analytics choice in the app. You can object to them at any time: turn off **Service analytics** in Settings → Privacy (Preferences in the mobile app), or write to [support@wonder.money](mailto:support@wonder.money). From then on none are recorded for your account, and any not yet sent are deleted. A self-hosted server sends none of them unless its operator turns them on. The full list is published at [github.com/wondermoney/wondermoney](https://github.com/wondermoney/wondermoney/blob/develop/docs/analytics-events.md).

**Usage events.** Browser analytics loads only after you opt in. You can decline or withdraw this choice in Settings → Privacy. The web app then sends the name of each screen you open, never its address, and the usage events in the same published list, such as a step of onboarding or of an import, without identifying you by email; session recording is disabled. Feedback you send from the app goes to our support inbox by email; analytics records only its category.

On the wonder.money website, PostHog loads only after you allow **Analytics** in **Privacy choices**. The website's free tools and resource lists then send page views and which tool you used, which list entry or next step you followed, with the page's name; they never send the amounts you enter in a tool. When you then follow a link from one of those pages to create an account, the link names the page you came from, and, only if you have also allowed analytics in the web app, the web app adds that page to your new account's signup event, so that your first budget and first goal can be counted against it. On every page, PostHog's page views also record, as they always have, the address of the page that sent you there and the campaign parameters in the link. The website itself works out only which of five broad kinds of place that was: an AI assistant, a search engine, a social network, no other site, or any other site. Apart from PostHog's own storage, that kind is the only thing the website keeps in your browser for the rest of the visit, and the only thing it passes to the web app, which adds it to your signup event on the same condition as the page. This tells us which free tools, and which kinds of sites, lead people to use WonderMoney. It is kept in PostHog, not with your account data, and carries no email address, name, amounts or other content you entered.

For more information about PostHog, please visit their Privacy Policy: [https://posthog.com/privacy](https://posthog.com/privacy)
