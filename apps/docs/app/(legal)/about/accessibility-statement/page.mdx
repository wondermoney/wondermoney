# Accessibility Statement

**Radix Catch, Lda** is committed to making `wonder.money` and `app.wonder.money` accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**, which is the standard referenced by the EU Web Accessibility Directive and the EU Accessibility Act. This is an ongoing effort, and we are actively working to identify and fix accessibility issues across the site and app.

---

## 2. Measures We Take

- Semantic HTML and heading structure to support screen readers and keyboard navigation.
- Sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, and interactive components.
- Descriptive `alt` text for meaningful images.
- Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes and zoom levels.

---

## 3. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. We are aware that accessibility is a continuous process, not a one-time fix, and we prioritize fixes as they are identified — whether through internal review, automated testing, or user reports.

---

## 4. Feedback and Contact

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using `wonder.money` or `app.wonder.money`, please let us know:

- Email: [support@wonder.money](mailto:support@wonder.money)

Please include the page or feature where you encountered the issue and, if possible, the assistive technology you were using. We aim to respond and address reported issues as quickly as reasonably possible.

---

## 5. Alternative Access

If you are unable to access information or complete a task on our website or app due to a disability, contact us at the email above and we will work with you to provide the information or service through an alternative method.

---

## 6. Governing Language

This document is published in English, Portuguese and Spanish. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the **English version** prevails.
