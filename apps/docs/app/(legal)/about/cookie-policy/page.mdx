# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how **Radix Catch, Lda** ("WonderMoney", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar storage technologies (such as `localStorage`) on `wonder.money` and `app.wonder.money`, and the choices available to you.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as browser `localStorage`, store small amounts of data locally in your browser. We use both interchangeably in this policy to mean any client-side storage mechanism.

---

## 2. Your Choices

When you first visit `wonder.money`, a consent banner asks you to choose which categories of cookies to allow. Your choice is stored in your browser and respected on every subsequent visit until you change it. You can update your choice at any time using the "Privacy choices" link in the site footer.

We operate on a **default-deny** basis: only Necessary storage is active until you explicitly opt in to Analytics or Advertising.

---

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Necessary

Required for the site to function. These cannot be disabled.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `wm_first_touch` | Remembers the campaign/referrer that first brought you to the site, so a signup can be attributed correctly. Contains no personal data — only marketing parameters (e.g. UTM values). | 30 minutes |
| Consent choice (`localStorage`) | Remembers your cookie preferences. | Until cleared by you |

### 3.2 Analytics (optional)

Active only if you opt in. Helps us understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Google Tag Manager (container `GTM-MGJJLRNH`) | Loads analytics tags. GTM itself sets no cookies directly; the tags it loads (e.g. Google Analytics) may set their own cookies once you've consented. |

### 3.3 Advertising (optional)

Active only if you opt in. Allows us to measure the performance of advertising campaigns (e.g. Meta/Facebook ads).

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Meta Pixel | Reads/sets `_fbc` and `_fbp` browser identifiers to measure ad campaign performance and attribute conversions. Only loaded after you grant Advertising consent. |

---

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties whose services are embedded in our site (for example, analytics or advertising providers listed above). We do not control these cookies directly; please refer to each provider's own privacy and cookie policy for details.

---

## 5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

In addition to the consent banner, most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking Necessary cookies may affect how the site functions.

---

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "last modified" date on this page.
